What is modelled

Every stage is a netlist of the parts on the schematic, solved sample by sample as a nonlinear circuit (nodal analysis with Newton iteration). Nothing is a waveshaper: clipping, rail sticking and recovery come out of the transistors and op-amps.

  1. Line jack J68: C142/C126, 5K62 series (R220/R212), 1K50 shunt (R197/R168) and the phantom feed resistors R523/R524, a −14.9 dB pad, driven unbalanced.
  2. Preamp: Q24/Q8 2SA1084 input pair (Ebers-Moll with Early effect), Q63/Q64/Q80 MMBT4403 current sources and reference, D80 1N4148 bias string, servo amps U17A/U25A, and the gain leg C1 1000 µF + trim + R1 3.32 Ω.
  3. U33A differential amp (R492/R425/R370/R482).
  4. Main board coupling C94/R167, C121/R137, and the lo cut U104A when switched in.
  5. EQ: the full mid (U101A/B with R120/R121 and C114/C115) and the hi/low Baxandall around U105A, set flat.
  6. Fader at U: R107 wiper into U103A, gain 1 + 1K1/510.

Op-amps are a macromodel built from datasheet figures: gain-bandwidth, open-loop gain, output swing and output resistance. Values that are not in the manual or a datasheet are stated as assumptions in the README: the 2SA1084 saturation current, the trim pot's taper, the op-amp output resistance, and 0 dBFS = +18 dBu. Parts whose effect is above 90 kHz (small compensation caps, L1) are left out. Live playback runs the circuit at the device sample rate.
