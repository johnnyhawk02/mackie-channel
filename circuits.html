What is modelled

Each circuit is a netlist of the real parts, solved sample by sample with the same nonlinear circuit solver as the Mackie channel. Nothing is a waveshaper. Auto level keeps the loudness about where the dry signal was, so you can switch circuits and compare tone rather than volume.

  1. Mackie CR1604-VLZ: the existing channel model, unchanged.
  2. Tube Screamer (TS808): 2SC1815 input buffer, JRC4558 non-inverting clipper with 1N914 diodes in the feedback, the 4K7 + 47 nF high-pass that gives the mid hump, tone stage at noon, output buffer. Drive is the 500K DRIVE pot.
  3. Fuzz Face: two AC128 germanium PNPs on 9 V positive ground, 100K DC feedback, 1K FUZZ pot with its 20 µF cap, fed from a passive pickup through a 250K volume pot. Drive turns the FUZZ pot and the guitar volume up together, which is how a Fuzz Face cleans up and breaks up.
  4. Tweed Champ (Fender 5F1 preamp): two 12AX7 triode stages (Koren model with grid current) on 250 V, 100K plates, 1K5 cathodes, the 1M VOLUME pot between them, into the 6V6 grid with its grid-current clamp. Drive is the VOLUME pot. The 6V6 power stage and output transformer are not modelled.

0 dBFS is taken as 1 V peak at the pedal or amp input (a hot pickup), and +18 dBu for the Mackie. Assumed values are listed in the source.
